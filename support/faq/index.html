Frequently asked questions

Short answers drawn from the product, security and legal pages.

KS_Link

What is KS_Link?

KS_Link is a free, end-to-end encrypted messenger for Windows, Linux and Android. It is KANASAKA's first release and is currently in alpha. More in the product overview and the technical overview.

Which platforms are supported?

Windows 10/11 (64-bit installer), Linux (.deb, .rpm and AppImage) and Android (arm64 APK). iOS and iPad support is planned; there is no iOS build to download yet. Installers are on the downloads page.

Can KANASAKA read my messages?

No. Messages are encrypted on the sending device, using per-conversation keys derived from each participant's device keys, before they reach the relay server. The relay stores and forwards encrypted payloads, conversation membership and delivery metadata such as timestamps and delivery acknowledgements. It has no access to message content. While a recipient is offline, the relay holds the still-encrypted message for up to 24 hours and deletes it as soon as it is delivered.

Do I need a kanasaka.com account to use KS_Link?

Yes. A KS_Link account is a KANASAKA account: you create it and sign in to it right in the KS_Link app (the app talks to kanasaka.com directly, so KS_Link's own servers never see your password), and you manage it — password, two-factor sign-in, deletion — from your Settings on kanasaka.com. KS_Link keeps conversations and social data on its own infrastructure, and kanasaka.com has no access to message content.

KS_Link accounts created before this change have no password of their own any more. Create a KANASAKA account (or sign in to yours) with the same email address you used in KS_Link and your conversations and posts are picked up automatically.

Is everything in KS_Link end-to-end encrypted?

Messages, including images, are. KS_Social (feed, stories, explore, reels, saved posts and profiles) is built into KS_Link and is not end-to-end encrypted by design: it is meant to be visible to your contacts or, where you choose, to everyone. Stories disappear after 24 hours, and the author of a story can see who viewed it.

Where is my message history stored?

Only on your own device, if you leave history on. KS_Link keeps conversations, including photos, in an encrypted file there. It is never uploaded, does not sync to your other devices, and KANASAKA cannot read, recover or restore it. If the device or its secure storage is reset or lost, the history is gone.

In the app's Settings you can turn history off, have messages erased after 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days or 1 year, erase everything when you sign out, clear a single chat, or delete it all. Messages you sent are also stored on the recipient's device under that person's own settings.

How do I delete my KS_Link account?

Delete your KANASAKA account in Account Management on kanasaka.com: that also erases your KS_Link account and its data and signs you out everywhere. If you only want to remove your KS_Link data, use "Delete my KS_Link data" in the app's Settings (Account). Export your data first if you want a copy.

Why does my system warn me when I install it?

The alpha builds are not signed with a store or code-signing certificate. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning: choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”. On Android the APK is self-signed, so you need to allow installing from unknown sources (or install it with ADB). The details are in the install notes on the downloads page.

How do I update KS_Link?

The Windows build updates itself in the app: Settings → Account → Check for updates. For Linux and Android, download the newer build from the downloads page.

Will things change?

Yes. KS_Link is early, active-development software. Features, APIs and the protocol itself may change between releases without backward compatibility.

kanasaka.com accounts

Which cookies does the website use?

One strictly necessary session cookie to keep you signed in. There are no tracking or advertising cookies. Details are in the Cookie Policy.

How are kanasaka.com accounts protected?

Passwords are hashed with Argon2id and checked against a policy that blocks short and common passwords. Sessions use Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict cookies. Email verification is required before sign-in, sign-in attempts are rate limited, and two-factor authentication with one-time codes and backup codes is available. The full list is on the security page.

How is my personal data handled?

The Privacy Policy explains what is processed and why, including a separate section for KS_Link.

Security and contact

How do I report a security problem?

Email contactns@kanasaka.com with enough detail to reproduce the issue, and please avoid accessing data that is not yours. We typically respond within one to two business days. Our machine-readable contact details are in security.txt.

Is the website down?

The system status page checks the website, the account database and the installer downloads when you open it.

Where is KANASAKA based, and how do I reach you?

KANASAKA is based in Germany. The legal details are in the Impressum, and general questions go through the contact page.
