KS_Package Pricing

Effective date: 21 August 2026 · kanasaka.com

1. Overview

KS_Package is the recurring KANASAKA subscription that grants entitlement to software and downloads made available under the Terms of Service while your subscription is active or trialing.

Subscriptions are billed per KANASAKA account ID. Access follows your account, not a single-machine license.

2. List prices

Plan Price Billing
KS_Package Monthly €10.00 Per month, recurring
KS_Package Annual €100.00 Per year, recurring

The price shown at checkout is the price that applies to your purchase. Prices may change for future billing periods with reasonable notice where required by law.

3. Included entitlement

  • KS Stocks — desktop market analytics and portfolio tooling (requires your own Alpaca and Ollama accounts).
  • Product downloads — authenticated access to entitled builds such as KS Unify while subscribed.
  • Account-based access — entitlement applies on every device where you sign in with your KANASAKA account.
  • Self-serve billing — upgrade, cancel, or update payment details from Account Settings via Stripe Customer Portal.

KANASAKA does not include Alpaca, Ollama, or brokerage services with KS_Package. Certain products (including private alpha releases) may have additional account or platform restrictions.

4. Payment and availability

Payments are processed by Stripe. KANASAKA does not store full payment card numbers on its servers.

Current status: KS_Package subscriptions are temporarily unavailable while KS Stocks completes approval. When subscriptions reopen, checkout will be available from the KS Stocks product page and Account Settings billing section.
