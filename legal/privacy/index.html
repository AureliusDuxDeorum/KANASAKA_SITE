Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how KANASAKA (“we”, “us”) processes personal data when you use kanasaka.com, create an account, download software, or contact us. We process data in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the GDPR where it applies. Section 11 separately covers KS_Link, which uses your KANASAKA account for identity but processes its own messaging and social data independently, as described there.

2. Data Controller

Controller: KANASAKA
Email: contactns@kanasaka.com
Website: https://kanasaka.com

3. Data We Collect

  • Account data: email address, password hash, optional display name, profile avatar, email verification status.
  • Security data: session identifiers, authentication events, optional two-factor phone number if you enable SMS 2FA.
  • Usage data: IP address, browser/device information, and request metadata processed by our hosting provider for security and delivery.
  • Communications: messages you send us and email delivery metadata when we send account or legal notices.

4. Purposes and Legal Bases

  • Provide the service (account creation, login, downloads, contact access) — contract performance and legitimate interests.
  • Security and fraud prevention — legitimate interests and legal obligations.
  • Transactional email (verification, password reset, requested legal documents) — contract performance and consent where required.
  • Legal compliance — legal obligation.

5. Processors and Hosting

We use trusted infrastructure providers to operate the Site, including Cloudflare (hosting, security, database, object storage), Resend (transactional email), and Twilio (SMS verification when 2FA is enabled). These providers process data on our behalf under appropriate contractual safeguards.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

We use a strictly necessary session cookie for authenticated access. Your browser may store local preferences such as theme selection and Terms acceptance state. We do not use third-party advertising cookies on the Site at this time. See our Cookie Policy for full details.

7. Retention

We retain account data while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward for security, backup, and legal purposes. Security logs are retained for a limited period. You may request account deletion through your account settings.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, and to data portability. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact us at contactns@kanasaka.com to exercise your rights.

9. International Transfers

Our providers may process data in countries outside your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or equivalent mechanisms.

10. Children

The Site is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16.

11. KS_Link

KS_Link is a free, alpha-stage messaging application we distribute from this Site. A KS_Link account is a KANASAKA account — you sign in to KS_Link with your KANASAKA credentials rather than a separate KS_Link password, and your account identity (handle, email address, display name) is created, managed, and deleted through this Site. KS_Link still runs its own separate infrastructure for everything that identity is used for — conversations, messages, and social data — and this section describes how that infrastructure processes your data, not the rest of this Privacy Policy’s description of the Site itself.

Linking is a one-time authorization: when you connect a KS_Link account to a KANASAKA account, KS_Link receives your account identifier, email address, and display name from this Site — nothing more, and it is sent to KS_Link’s own relay, never to a third party. KS_Link encrypts message content end-to-end on your device before it reaches our relay; we cannot read your messages. Our relay does process data needed to deliver the service: your linked account identifier, conversation membership (who you have messaged, not what was said), device public keys needed for encryption, and message delivery metadata (timestamps and delivery acknowledgements) without the message content itself. While a recipient is offline, the relay holds the still-encrypted message for up to 24 hours (at most 50 messages per person) and deletes it as soon as it is delivered; it keeps no copy afterwards. An account created before account linking existed keeps its own KS_Link password until it links. KS_Link also sends typing indicators and read receipts to the person you are chatting with while you chat; both can be turned off in Settings, in which case your device never sends them. The social features built into KS_Link (feed, stories, comments, likes) are not end-to-end encrypted — visibility is controlled instead, limited to your contacts unless you choose to post publicly — stories disappear after 24 hours, and the author of a story can see who viewed it.

Message history on your device. KS_Link can keep a copy of your conversations, including photos you send and receive, in an encrypted file on the device you use, so they are still there when you reopen the app. That copy exists only on that device: it is encrypted with a key generated on the device and held in the operating system’s secure storage; it is never uploaded to our relay or anywhere else; KS_Link does not back it up or sync it; and your other devices will not have it. We cannot read, recover or delete it, and because we never receive it we do not process it — you control it entirely. In the app’s Settings (Account, Privacy) you can turn history off, have messages erased automatically after 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days or 1 year, erase everything when you sign out, clear a single chat, or delete all of it; deleting your account also erases it from the device on which you do so. Up to 5,000 messages are kept per conversation. If the device or its secure storage is reset or lost, the history is unrecoverable by design. Messages you sent are also stored on the recipient’s device under that person’s own settings; deleting your copy does not delete theirs. Photos sent in chats are resized and compressed on your device before sending (long edge up to 1600 px, about 500 KB).

If you report content or an account within KS_Link, we retain the report and limited associated records for a period (at least 90 days) to support a review process and to meet legal reporting obligations that may apply to online messaging services, including reporting apparent child sexual abuse material to the relevant authorities where required by law. For a linked account, delete your KANASAKA account from this Site’s Account Management settings to end your KS_Link sign-in; KS_Link’s own conversation and social data is not automatically deleted by that action today and should be exported or deleted separately from within the app’s own Settings beforehand, or by contacting us. An account that hasn’t linked yet can still export or permanently delete its KS_Link account and data entirely from within the app’s own Settings.

We process this data to provide the service (contract performance) and to meet legal obligations such as abuse reporting. The rights described in Section 8 apply equally to the KS_Link data we hold — use the in-app export/delete tools for the fastest path, or contact us at contactns@kanasaka.com. Message history stored on your device is not data we hold, so we cannot export or erase it for you; the in-app controls above are the way to do both.
