1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how KANASAKA (“we”, “us”) processes personal data when you use kanasaka.com, create an account, download software, or contact us. We process data in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the GDPR where it applies. Section 11 separately covers KS_Link, which runs its own account system distinct from the Site’s accounts described below.

2. Data Controller

Controller: KANASAKA
Email: contactns@kanasaka.com
Website: https://kanasaka.com

3. Data We Collect

  • Account data: email address, password hash, optional display name, profile avatar, email verification status.
  • Security data: session identifiers, authentication events, optional two-factor phone number if you enable SMS 2FA.
  • Usage data: IP address, browser/device information, and request metadata processed by our hosting provider for security and delivery.
  • Communications: messages you send us and email delivery metadata when we send account or legal notices.

4. Purposes and Legal Bases

  • Provide the service (account creation, login, downloads, contact access) — contract performance and legitimate interests.
  • Security and fraud prevention — legitimate interests and legal obligations.
  • Transactional email (verification, password reset, requested legal documents) — contract performance and consent where required.
  • Legal compliance — legal obligation.

5. Processors and Hosting

We use trusted infrastructure providers to operate the Site, including Cloudflare (hosting, security, database, object storage), Resend (transactional email), and Twilio (SMS verification when 2FA is enabled). These providers process data on our behalf under appropriate contractual safeguards.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

We use a strictly necessary session cookie for authenticated access. Your browser may store local preferences such as theme selection and Terms acceptance state. We do not use third-party advertising cookies on the Site at this time. See our Cookie Policy for full details.

7. Retention

We retain account data while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward for security, backup, and legal purposes. Security logs are retained for a limited period. You may request account deletion through your account settings.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, and to data portability. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact us at contactns@kanasaka.com to exercise your rights.

9. International Transfers

Our providers may process data in countries outside your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or equivalent mechanisms.

10. Children

The Site is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16.

11. KS_Link

KS_Link is a free, alpha-stage messaging application we distribute from this Site. It is a separate service from kanasaka.com with its own account system — registering in KS_Link does not create an account on this Site, and this section describes KS_Link’s own data practices, not the rest of this Privacy Policy’s description of the Site itself.

KS_Link encrypts message content end-to-end on your device before it reaches our relay; we cannot read your messages. Our relay does process data needed to deliver the service: your KS_Link account (handle, email address, password hash), conversation membership (who you have messaged, not what was said), device public keys needed for encryption, and message delivery metadata (timestamps and delivery acknowledgements) without the message content itself. If you use the optional KS_Social feature, the posts, comments, and likes you create there are not end-to-end encrypted — visibility is controlled instead, limited to your contacts.

If you report content or an account within KS_Link, we retain the report and limited associated records for a period (at least 90 days) to support a review process and to meet legal reporting obligations that may apply to online messaging services, including reporting apparent child sexual abuse material to the relevant authorities where required by law. You can export or permanently delete your KS_Link account and its data at any time from within the app’s own Settings; deleting your KS_Link account does not affect any separate account you may have on this Site, and vice versa.

We process this data to provide the service (contract performance) and to meet legal obligations such as abuse reporting. The rights described in Section 8 apply equally to KS_Link data — use the in-app export/delete tools for the fastest path, or contact us at contactns@kanasaka.com.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.

13. Contact

Privacy inquiries: contactns@kanasaka.com
