Refund Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

1. Overview

This Refund Policy explains when subscription fees paid to KANASAKA (“we”, “us”) are refundable. It supplements our Terms of Service, which governs your subscription — read both together.

2. General rule: non-refundable

Except where mandatory consumer law requires otherwise, subscription fees are non-refundable, including for partial billing periods, unused time, or if you simply stop using the service without cancelling. Cancelling stops future renewals — it does not retroactively refund the current billing period, which you keep access to until it ends.

3. EU / EEA 14-day withdrawal right

If you are a consumer in the European Union or European Economic Area, you may have a statutory right to withdraw from a distance contract within 14 days of purchase, without giving a reason.

If you request immediate access to digital content or services and expressly consent to begin performance before the 14-day period ends, you acknowledge that — to the extent permitted by law — you may lose this withdrawal right once delivery/performance has begun, i.e. once you start using the service.

If you have not yet started using the service and are within the 14-day window, contact contactns@kanasaka.com to exercise your withdrawal right.

4. Failed or disputed payments

If a renewal payment fails, we may suspend or end entitlement until payment is successfully processed — this is not a refund situation, since no successful charge occurred for the suspended period. If you believe you were charged in error (e.g. a duplicate charge, or a charge after you successfully cancelled before renewal), contact us and we will investigate and correct genuine billing errors.

5. Other mandatory consumer rights

Nothing in this policy limits any refund, cancellation, or warranty right you have under mandatory consumer protection law in your jurisdiction that cannot be waived by agreement. Where such law grants you a right beyond what is described above, that law controls.

6. How to request something covered by this policy

Email contactns@kanasaka.com with your account email and the reason for your request. We aim to respond within a reasonable time and will tell you clearly whether your situation is covered by section 3, 4, or 5 above.

7. Changes

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
