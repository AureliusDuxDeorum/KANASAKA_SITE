Cookie Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026

1. What this page covers

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and local-storage data kanasaka.com — the website — uses. It supplements our Privacy Policy.

KS_Link, the messaging application you can download from this Site, is a native desktop and mobile app, not a website, and does not use cookies at all. Its session token is stored in your operating system’s own secure credential store (Windows Credential Manager, the Linux Secret Service, or the Android Keystore, depending on platform) rather than in a cookie or browser storage. See Section 11 of the Privacy Policy for what KS_Link itself processes.

2. Strictly necessary cookies

We set one session cookie when you sign in, used solely to keep you authenticated. The Site cannot function (you cannot stay logged in) without it, so it is set automatically and is not subject to the consent banner's choice — this matches the "strictly necessary" exemption under the ePrivacy Directive and GDPR, which does not require consent for cookies essential to a service you've requested.

3. Local storage (not a cookie, but disclosed here)

Your browser's local storage, not a cookie, stores a few preferences entirely on your device: your selected theme, and whether you've already acknowledged this cookie notice. None of this is sent to our servers, and none of it is used for tracking or advertising.

4. No third-party advertising or analytics cookies

We do not use third-party advertising, cross-site tracking, or analytics cookies on the Site at this time. If that changes, we will update this page and, where legally required, ask for your consent before setting any non-essential cookie.

5. Managing cookies

Because we only set a strictly necessary session cookie, there is no granular opt-in/opt-out to offer beyond the notice banner's acknowledgement — blocking it in your browser will simply sign you out. You can clear cookies and local storage at any time through your browser's settings.

6. Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy if what we use changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
