KS_Link

KS_Link is a free, end-to-end encrypted messaging application, built as a Tauri desktop app (Windows, Linux) and a native Android app, with iOS planned. It is currently in alpha.

Messaging

Messages are encrypted on the sending device before they ever reach KANASAKA's relay server, using per-conversation keys derived from each participant's device keys. The relay stores and forwards encrypted payloads, conversation membership, and delivery metadata (timestamps, delivery acknowledgements) — it has no access to message content. Images are sent through the same end-to-end encrypted channel as text.

Account system

KS_Link runs its own self-hosted account system, entirely independent of kanasaka.com. Registering in KS_Link does not create a KANASAKA_SITE account, and a KANASAKA_SITE account does not give you a KS_Link account — the two systems share no login, no database, and no subscription. KS_Link does not require, and is not gated behind, a KS_Package subscription.

Account security includes TOTP-based two-factor authentication with backup codes for account recovery, and a session list that lets you see every device currently signed in and revoke any of them individually.

Plugin system

Optional features run as plugins inside a WASM sandbox with an explicit, revocable permission model — a plugin only gets the data access you grant it, and that access can be withdrawn at any time. The first plugin is KS_Social, an opt-in feed where contacts can share posts; unlike direct messages, KS_Social content is not end-to-end encrypted, since it is meant to be visible to your contacts rather than private between two people.

Moderation

You can block a contact at any time, and report a message, post, or account for review. Reported content and limited associated records are retained to support that review and to meet legal reporting obligations that apply to messaging services.

Alpha status

KS_Link is early, active-development software. Features, APIs, and the protocol itself may change between releases without backward compatibility. See the Privacy Policy for how KS_Link handles your data, and the Terms of Service for the alpha-software terms that apply to it.
