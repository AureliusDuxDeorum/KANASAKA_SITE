KS_Link

KS_Link is a free, end-to-end encrypted messaging application, built as a Tauri desktop app (Windows, Linux) and a native Android app, with iOS planned. It is currently in alpha.

Messaging

Messages are encrypted on the sending device before they ever reach KANASAKA's relay server, using per-conversation keys derived from each participant's device keys. The relay stores and forwards encrypted payloads, conversation membership, and delivery metadata (timestamps, delivery acknowledgements) — it has no access to message content. Images are sent through the same end-to-end encrypted channel as text, after being resized and compressed on the sending device to fit within a message (long edge up to 1600 px, about 500 KB). While a recipient is offline the relay holds the still-encrypted message for up to 24 hours and deletes it on delivery.

History on your device

KS_Link can keep your conversations, including photos, in an encrypted file on your own device so they survive closing the app. Each record is encrypted with a random per-account key held in the operating system’s secure storage; nothing is uploaded and nothing syncs between devices, so the relay never sees your history. You can turn history off, set automatic deletion (24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, 1 year), erase it on sign-out, or delete it at any time from Settings. If the device’s secure storage is lost the history cannot be recovered. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Account system

A KS_Link account is a KANASAKA_SITE account: you sign in to KS_Link with your KANASAKA_SITE credentials, and the account itself is created, managed, and deleted from your Settings on this Site rather than inside the app. KS_Link stores its own conversation, message-delivery, and social data separately — linking identity does not give KANASAKA_SITE access to message content, which stays end-to-end encrypted as described above. KS_Link does not require, and is not gated behind, a KS_Package subscription. Accounts created before this change keep working with their original KS_Link password during a transition period and are offered the option to link to a KANASAKA_SITE account the next time they sign in.

Account security includes TOTP-based two-factor authentication with backup codes for account recovery (for accounts still on the legacy, unlinked login), and a session list that lets you see every device currently signed in to KS_Link and revoke any of them individually.

Social

KS_Social — a feed, stories, explore, reels, saved posts and your profile — is built directly into KS_Link and is the app’s landing page, with chat alongside it. Unlike direct messages, social content is not end-to-end encrypted, since it is meant to be visible to your contacts (or, where you choose, to everyone) rather than private between two people. Stories disappear after 24 hours and their author can see who viewed them. Typing indicators and read receipts in chats can be turned off in Settings.

Moderation

You can block a contact at any time, and report a message, post, or account for review. Reported content and limited associated records are retained to support that review and to meet legal reporting obligations that apply to messaging services.

Alpha status

KS_Link is early, active-development software. Features, APIs, and the protocol itself may change between releases without backward compatibility. See the Privacy Policy for how KS_Link handles your data, and the Terms of Service for the alpha-software terms that apply to it.
