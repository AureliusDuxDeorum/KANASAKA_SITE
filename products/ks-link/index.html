KS_Link Alpha

A free, end-to-end encrypted messenger for Windows, Linux and Android.

Download Technical overview

How it works

Private by construction

What KS_Link does, and what the relay server can and cannot see.

  • End-to-end encrypted messages

    Messages are encrypted on the sending device, using per-conversation keys derived from each participant's device keys, before they reach the relay. Images travel through the same encrypted channel as text.

  • A relay without access to content

    The relay stores and forwards encrypted payloads, conversation membership and delivery metadata (timestamps, delivery acknowledgements). It has no access to message content. While a recipient is offline it holds the still-encrypted message for up to 24 hours and deletes it on delivery.

  • History stays on your device

    Conversations, including photos, can be kept in an encrypted file on the device only. It is never uploaded and does not sync to other devices. You can turn it off or set it to erase itself after 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days or 1 year.

  • One KANASAKA account

    A KS_Link account is a KANASAKA account: you sign in with your KANASAKA credentials. KS_Link keeps conversations and social data on its own infrastructure, and linking does not give kanasaka.com access to message content.

  • Account security

    Your KANASAKA account's sign-in protections apply, and a session list in the app shows every signed-in device and lets you revoke each one individually. Older KS_Link accounts keep their original sign-in, with TOTP two-factor authentication, during a transition period.

  • Social built in

    KS_Social (feed, stories, explore, reels, saved posts and your profile) is part of KS_Link. Unlike direct messages, social content is not end-to-end encrypted by design: it is meant to be visible to your contacts or, where you choose, to everyone.

  • Typing and read receipts are optional

    Typing indicators and read receipts are sent to the person you are chatting with only while the settings allow it. You can turn both off in Settings.

  • Blocking and reporting

    You can block a contact at any time and report a message, post or account for review. Reported content and limited associated records are retained to support review and to meet legal reporting obligations.

Alpha software

KS_Link is in early, active development. Features, APIs and the protocol itself may change between releases without backward compatibility. See the Privacy Policy for how KS_Link handles your data and the Terms of Service for the alpha-software terms.
