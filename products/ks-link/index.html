How it works

Private by construction

What KS_Link does, and what the relay server can and cannot see.

  • End-to-end encrypted messages

    Messages are encrypted on the sending device, using per-conversation keys derived from each participant's device keys, before they reach the relay. Images travel through the same encrypted channel as text.

  • A relay without access to content

    The relay stores and forwards encrypted payloads, conversation membership and delivery metadata (timestamps, delivery acknowledgements). It has no access to message content.

  • Independent accounts

    KS_Link runs its own self-hosted account system, separate from kanasaka.com: no shared login, database or subscription. It is not gated behind a KS_Package subscription.

  • Account security

    TOTP two-factor authentication with backup codes for recovery, and a session list that shows every signed-in device and lets you revoke each one individually.

  • Sandboxed plugins

    Optional features run in a WASM sandbox with explicit, revocable permissions. The first plugin, KS_Social, is an opt-in feed; unlike direct messages, its content is not end-to-end encrypted by design.

  • Blocking and reporting

    You can block a contact at any time and report a message, post or account for review. Reported content and limited associated records are retained to support review and to meet legal reporting obligations.
