Desktop & Mobile

Encrypted Messaging, Free Alpha

KS_Link is a Tauri desktop and Android app for direct, end-to-end encrypted messaging — text and images, with a WASM-sandboxed plugin system (starting with KS_Social, an opt-in feed for contacts). It runs its own self-hosted account system, entirely separate from KANASAKA_SITE accounts and KS_Package — there is nothing to subscribe to.

Capabilities

End-to-End Encryption

Messages are encrypted on your device before they ever reach our relay — the server cannot read message content.

Two-Factor Authentication

TOTP-based 2FA with backup codes, plus session management so you can review and revoke access from other devices.

Image Sharing

Send and receive images in a conversation, encrypted the same way as text.

Sandboxed Plugins

Optional features (like the KS_Social feed) run in a WASM sandbox with explicit, revocable permissions — never silent access to your data.

Blocking & Reporting

Block a contact at any time, or report a post or account for review.

Independent Account System

KS_Link accounts are separate from KANASAKA_SITE and KS_Package — registering in the app doesn't create a kanasaka.com account, and vice versa.

Alpha software

KS_Link is early, active-development software. Features, APIs, and the protocol itself may change between releases without backward compatibility. See the Privacy Policy for how KS_Link handles your data, and the Terms of Service for the alpha-software terms that apply to it.
